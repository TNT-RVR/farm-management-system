# Week of November 2, 2026

44 changes to the app this week.

## New

- **Cattle:** The benchmark the cost form offers is Alberta's
- **Map:** Each Compare pane opens with its field just fitting, and keeps a hand-set view
- **Map:** Compare names the crop on every field
- **Research:** A library of papers on our crops, chemicals and water
- **Research:** Keep the agronomy, drop the toxicology
- **Research:** A Research view, so the library can be read
- **Research:** Ask the app can read the papers and say which one
- **Satellite:** Canola bloom alert and flower pictures
- **Scale:** Live weight off the yard scale, and full/empty taken straight off it
- **Settings:** Names and defaults is gone; each of its six goes where it belongs
- **Settings:** The contact address goes back, beside the name and logo

## Changed

- **Partner:** His own grant and conference tracking; spray restrictions for his fields
- **Partner accounts step 1:** Fields, crops and extras shared per company
- **Partner accounts step 2:** Views from a preset plus per-company ticks
- **Partner accounts step 3:** Settings > Users & access > Partners
- **Partner accounts step 4:** Unfence the partner code for the public copy
- **Partner grants:** List shows (new unless closed), detail read-only
- **Partner preview:** Field progress reads his fields' rows
- **Partner preview:** Grant and conference lists read as his views give them
- **Partner round 4:** His own equipment, tasks and calendar; grants, conferences, fuel, weather, reports
- **Partners settings:** The preset's pages by their own names

## Fixed

- **Canola bloom:** Pull missing summer looks; ripening pods are not a start
- **Canola bloom:** Ripening cut-off also ends a bloom that never dropped below 10 %
- **Canola bloom card:** 'Last look' once flowering is over; gold see-through
- **Grazing:** The watch stops crashing on the browser's Supabase client
- **Health:** Allow for FieldNET's three-hourly winter sync
- **Irrigation:** The district is named on Setup, where it can be found
- **Map:** Compare panes resize with the grid instead of keeping their first canvas
- **Offline:** A partner preview's narrowed lists are never saved to the device
- **Offline:** An empty reference list is never treated as fresh
- **Research:** A variety belongs under its crop, not beside the next one
- **Research:** Open the tab on the click, not after the link is signed
- **Research:** Titles read as titles, not as markup
- **Research:** Rank a passage by how much of the question it answers
- **Research:** Search results in the order they answer the question
- **Research:** A cited paper's link opens that paper
- **Scale:** Link a capture to its load after the load is written
- **Sidebar:** The arrow narrows it to a bar of section icons instead of hiding it; a click on the bar widens it, a section icon opens that section, Home and Map go straight there

## Improved

- **Map:** Compare panes show in a second, not twenty
- **Research:** Ask the index for the paragraphs worth ranking
- **Research:** Keep a look-up well inside the eight-second cap
- **Research:** Survive the first search of the day

## Other changes

- Partner growing season and harvest views; hide his loads on our scale
- Partner field progress counts his crop-share acres

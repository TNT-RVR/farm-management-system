# Week of October 29, 2026

178 changes to the app this week.

## This week

**A new menu, grouped by season.** The old menu had one entry per feature
and had grown long. The same pages are now grouped the way a farm's year runs:
Planting, Growing, Harvest and Winter season, among the year-round sections
(Fields, Map, Financials, Cattle, Equipment, Tasks & calendar, Tools), with
Settings pinned at the bottom. Every view has one row of tabs, at most six,
with the rest behind a "+N ▾". A view used in more than one season is listed
in each, and a ↗ marks the shortcut copy. Several big pages were split into
smaller views: Fertilizer, Cattle, Harvest, Spraying and chemicals, Fuel and
trucking, and Settings. Old addresses and bookmarks still land in the right
place. Each person can choose their own sections, order and phone-bar buttons
for the phone and the computer under Settings → My account → Menu (where the
old menu can be switched back on, for now). An admin can still hide whole
parts of the app for everyone under Settings → Farm setup → Parts of the app.
README.md has a new "Finding your way" section.

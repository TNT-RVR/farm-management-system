# Week of October 6, 2026: the first public copy

The Farm Management System is now available to other farms.

It's a prototype with no warranty: read [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md)
before you rely on any figure or recommendation it gives you.

- **To install it:** follow [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md). It takes about an hour and
  costs nothing to try.
- **To see what it costs to run:** [docs/RUNNING-COSTS.md](../docs/RUNNING-COSTS.md)
  compares the free and paid plan of every service it uses.
- **To fit it to your farm:** start at **Settings → Farm setup** in the app.
  [docs/CUSTOMIZING.md](../docs/CUSTOMIZING.md) covers the few parts that need
  a code change.

From next week, each week's changes will be listed here and released as a
`.patch` file you can apply to your own copy.

# Taking part

This repository is a copy of a working farm's app, published every week.
Changes are made in the farm's own copy and flow out from there, so **pull
requests here can't be merged directly**. Please don't be put off by that.
There are better ways to help:

- **Ideas and requests:** start a thread in
  [Discussions → Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas). Say what problem
  on your farm it would solve, not only the feature.
- **Something you built:** show it in
  [Discussions → Show and tell](../../discussions/categories/show-and-tell),
  with a screenshot and, if you're willing, the patch or the prompt you gave
  your AI assistant. Good ones may be brought into the main app and credited
  in that week's notes.
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](../../issues) with what you did, what you
  expected and what happened. Screenshots help.
- **Stuck on setup:** [Discussions → Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a).

## Ground rules

- Be kind. Everyone here is running a farm first and writing software second.
- **Don't post your own farm's private details** in public threads:
  names, land locations, account numbers, prices you negotiated.
- By sharing code or a patch here you agree it can be used under this
  project's licence ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)).
